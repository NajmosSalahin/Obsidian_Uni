### **Page 1**

1. Explain the following terms along with examples: (i) Scaler and Unit Matrices, (ii) Incidence Matrix, (iii) Matrix Addition, (iv) Kronecker product, and (v) Hadamard product.
    
2. Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 1 \\ -3 & 2 & 1 \\ -2 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \\ 1 & 2 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$. Prove that (i) The transpose of any transposed matrix $A$ equals the original matrix, (ii) The transpose of a sum of two conformable matrices $A$ and $B$ is the sum of their transposes, and (iii) The transpose of the product of two conformable matrices $A$ and $B$ is the product of their transposes, but in reverse order.
    
3. Prove that the determinants of the transpose of a matrix equals the determinants of the matrix itself for the matrix $B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 2 & 7 & 2 \\ 4 & 4 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$. Also, show that if the two rows of a matrix are same, then the determinant of the matrix is zero.
    
4. Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 & 5 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 6 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 & 9 \\ 6 & 1 & 6 \\ 5 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. Show that (a) $|3A| = 3^3|A|$, (b) $|AB| = |A||B|$, and (c) $|A \otimes B| = |A|^3|B|^3$.
    
5. Let $A = \begin{bmatrix} 6 & -1 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ (a) Calculate the transpose of $A^{-1}$ and the inverse of $A'$, and (b) Calculate the inverse of $A^{-1}$.
    
6. Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times n$ matrices. Then the following relations hold, where $k_1$ and $k_2$ are arbitrary scalars. (i) $tr(k_1A + k_2B) = k_1tr(A) + k_2tr(B)$, and (ii) $tr(AB) = tr(BA)$.
    
7. Define the rank of a matrix. Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times k$ matrices, not necessarily of full rank. Then prove that (i) $\rho(A+B) \le \rho(A) + \rho(B)$, and (ii) $\rho(A-B) \ge |\rho(A) - \rho(B)|$.

    (ii) (ii) $dim(V) = dim[R(A)] + dim[N(A)]$, where $V$ is the domain of $T$.
