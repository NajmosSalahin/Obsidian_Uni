Linear Transformations:

> **Definition 3.16.** A transformation $T$ is said to be linear if $T$ transforms every vector $X \in E^{(n)}$ into a vector $T(X) \in E^{(m)}$ such that for any two vectors $X_1$ and $X_2 \in E^{(n)}$ and any two scalars $c_1$ and $c_2$, we have
> 
> $$T(c_1X_1 + c_2X_2) = c_1T(X_1) + c_2T(X_2) \quad$$
