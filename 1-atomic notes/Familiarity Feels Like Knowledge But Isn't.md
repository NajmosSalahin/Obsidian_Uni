## Familiarity Feels Like Knowledge But Isn't

The brain mistakes fluency for understanding. Something
practiced many times feels known — but small details
inside it get lost. Something learned an hour ago feels
uncertain — but is actually being actively encoded.
Familiarity deceives. Reconstruction reveals.
